OBSERVATION MOTIVATING THE PREDICTION
weightFast = 0.6066448974609373 and rateFast = 5.239865393555934 carry the
numeric signature of a grid-then-refine / bisection search rather than a
converged simplex or gradient optimum. The one-dimensional golden-section
routine used for the single-parameter models cannot search a two-parameter
surface, so the mixture (free: weightFast, rateFast; rateSlow constrained by the
mean-one condition) is the model most at risk of being sub-optimally fitted.

PREDICTION
P-B1. An independent fit of the SAME likelihood on the SAME training partition,
      using genuine multivariate optimizers with multiple starts, will find a
      training negative log-likelihood NO WORSE than the committed mixture fit.
P-B2. It will find a STRICTLY BETTER training NLL for the mixture, i.e. the
      committed mixture parameters are NOT the global optimum.
P-B3. The three single-parameter models (exponential fixed, Weibull shape,
      lognormal sigma) WILL be recovered to within optimizer tolerance, because
      golden-section is adequate on a unimodal one-dimensional surface.

IF P-B2 HOLDS, THE CONSEQUENCE IS SCIENTIFIC, NOT COSMETIC
Every model comparison involving M3 — including the headline adverse result that
the lognormal baseline out-scores the two-timescale mixture — would be a
comparison against a sub-optimally fitted M3. The adverse result could then be
an artifact of the fit rather than a property of the model class.

BOTH DIRECTIONS ARE ADVERSE AND BOTH MUST BE REPORTED
  - If the better fit does NOT overturn the lognormal result: the adverse
    finding is strengthened and survives a fairer comparison.
  - If the better fit DOES overturn it: the repository published an unreliable
    self-criticism. That is an integrity finding and must be reported in the
    integrity frame FIRST, before any scientific reframing.

FALSIFIER
If independent global optimization recovers the committed mixture parameters to
within tolerance and no better training NLL exists anywhere in the feasible
region, then the mixture IS globally fitted, P-B2 is refuted, and the lognormal
result rests on a fair comparison.
