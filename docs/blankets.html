The Blanket Builder

A Markov blanket is a boundary where something crosses — ions, force, a phosphoryl group, a ligand — and it is identified by the signals that gate the crossing: what opens it, what stops it. This workbench holds three columns: what our code actually instantiates today, what the cited biology suggests, and your workspace to build from an inventory where every signal carries a verbatim quote, a full APA citation, a mirrored copy, and a link to the original.

Language contract, binding on this page. We never write fact. We write confirmed observation, standing durable measure with observed evidence, reported measurement, stated mechanism, proposal, contested, not established here. Source hedging is preserved, never stripped.
