1 · The behaviour we must replicate — the yardstick

From the Wikipedia flagellum article and the model's own primary anchors (Antani 2021, Wadhwa 2022, Lo 2018, Mattingly & Tu 2026). Our model is an explicit teaching reduction of this, not a molecular replica. SCIENCE.md:24-42

Machine

  • Stator MotA₅:MotB₂, 11–16 units, proton-driven; more recruited under load
  • Rotor C-ring switch FliG/FliM/FliN
  • Rings L·P·MS · hook · flagellin filament (~20 nm, 11 protofilaments)
  • Stator↔rotor gear ratio ≈6.2

Physics

  • Energy = proton-motive force (Na⁺ in Vibrio)
  • Loaded 200–1000 rpm; unloaded 6,000–100,000 rpm
  • Stall torque ∝ stators × PMF; speed falls with load
  • Low-Reynolds regime

Behaviour

  • CCW = run (bundle, forward); CW = tumble (unbundle)
  • CheY-P → C-ring biases CW → tumbling
  • Run-and-tumble = biased random walk = chemotaxis
  • Adaptation via receptor methylation

The three species the truth contract keeps separate: E. coli (behavioural — run/tumble, PMF), Salmonella and Bacillus (structural — switch complex, protofilaments). They are evidence sources, never one measured specimen. CLAUDE.md:207-209 · lib/walkthrough.js:28,43,58
